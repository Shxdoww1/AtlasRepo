Tronium Power Technology (Real-World)

TRONIUM is a power conversion technology designed to solve energy-wasting "vampire loads" in electronics. Developed by Semitrex, it is a proprietary, ASIC-based power supply technology. Technology: It utilizes a Capacitive Voltage Reduction (CVR) method called Muxcapacitor PSSoC (Power Supply on a Chip). Efficiency: It claims over *90–95%* AC-DC efficiency at all loads. Application: It is intended to be embedded in consumer electronics (TVs, chargers), white goods, and IoT sensors to enhance energy efficiency. Status: As of mid-2023, the technology was in its final test phase (third-generation silicon) with multiple patents issued and pending.
